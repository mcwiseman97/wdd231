NP Membership Benefits

Cost: Free for qualifying non-profits

  • Directory listing
  • Access to networking events
  • Community partnership opportunities
  • Chamber newsletter subscription

Bronze Membership Benefits

Cost: $150 / year

  • Directory listing with logo
  • Member-rate event tickets
  • Access to training workshops
  • Chamber newsletter subscription

Silver Membership Benefits

Cost: $300 / year

  • Enhanced directory listing
  • Event discounts for staff
  • Quarterly advertising opportunities
  • Invitation to member-only mixers

Gold Membership Benefits

Cost: $500 / year

  • Premium directory placement
  • Home page spotlight eligibility
  • Priority event sponsorship options
  • Complimentary training seats
  • Ribbon cutting support
