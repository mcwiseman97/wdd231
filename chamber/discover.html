Explore the landmarks, parks, and community spaces that make Ellis County a great place to live, visit, and do business.

About the Area

  • Population of approximately 45,000 in the city of Waxahachie, with strong growth across Ellis County.
  • Located about 30 miles south of Dallas along I-35E an accessible hub for North Texas commerce.
  • Known for historic architecture, the Gingerbread Trail, and a vibrant downtown square.
  • Home to parks, Lake Waxahachie recreation, and year-round festivals that draw visitors and support local business.
